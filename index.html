PDF invoices to Excel

The problem
Invoices from three suppliers, each with a different layout, typed into a spreadsheet by hand every month.
What the script does
Finds each field by its label rather than its position, so layout differences don't break it. Checks that line items add up to the subtotal and that subtotal plus tax equals the total.
Result
Seven invoices in one workbook with an invoices sheet, a line-items sheet and a problems sheet. A scanned file that needed OCR was flagged instead of skipped.

Contact list cleanup and de-duplication

The problem
A customer list with stray spaces, mixed capitalization, three phone formats and the same people entered more than once.
What the script does
Normalizes names, emails, phones, states and dates. Matches duplicates by email regardless of case, or by name and phone, and copies any missing details into the row that's kept.
Result
26 rows down to 20, invalid emails highlighted, and a "Removed Rows" sheet explaining why each of the 6 removed rows went.

Orders spreadsheet to branded PDF invoices

The problem

What the script does
Groups orders by customer, even when names are typed inconsistently, and produces one invoice each with the business's colors, tax, due date and payment terms. Business details live in one settings file.

Four finished invoices and a summary CSV from a single run.
